# Light Your Way

This is my first attempt at creating a GitHub repository of this kind.

The idea was inspired by https://github.com/H3Art-q/Gitbook-CSTguidance (Jinan University CST Guide), which itself drew from the legendary Shanghai Jiao Tong University survival manual: https://survivesjtu.gitbook.io/survivesjtumanual/.

At the beginning, it was just a personal place to jot down notes — almost like a diary — about my growth, struggles, and lessons learned in university. Like many freshmen, I felt completely lost: confused about courses, campus life, relationships, and my future… everything. I didn’t know how to study anymore. Should I keep doing what worked in high school, or become someone entirely new?

I had (and still have) a lot of thoughts I wanted to get out.

So I started writing. I hope one day this repository can grow into a meaningful guide that helps you when you’re deeply unsure about what you really want — just like I was.

I also hope you’ll contribute your own experiences and opinions, no matter how small. Every voice makes this project better.

**As the name suggests, my biggest wish is that this little project can light up at least a small part of your journey — and keep shining for every student who comes after us.**
